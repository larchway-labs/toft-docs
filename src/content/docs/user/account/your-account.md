---
title: Your account
description: Your role, everything you can reach, your API keys and your sign-ins.
---

Open **Your account** from the menu under your name, top right.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your role, your groups, and [Request publisher access](../becoming-a-publisher/) |
| **Access** | Everything you can reach: apps, groups, storage grants |
| **Storage** | [Storage](../../data/browsing-a-store/) you hold a grant on (installs with storage only) |
| **Credentials** | Your [API keys](../../cli/api-keys/) (where personal keys are enabled) |
| **Security** | Sign-in providers, recent sign-ins, password, **Sign out everywhere** |

:::tip
Check **Access** before asking for something — you may already have it.
:::

**Sign out everywhere** ends every session, this one included. Your API keys keep working; revoke them on **Credentials** if needed.
