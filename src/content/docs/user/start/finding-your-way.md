Four places cover almost everything you do in Toft.

## The app list

Where you land after signing in.

- **Mine** — apps you own, a group you belong to owns, or that were shared
  with you.
- **All apps** — every app on the install whose name you may see.

An app you can see but not open is listed so you can
[ask the owner for access](../../share/requesting-access/). Filter by status,
[tag](../../share/tags/) and more; search by name.

## An app's page

Tabs appear according to your access and what the install has enabled. A tab
for something your install does not have is absent, not disabled.

| Tab | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Is it running, its URL, the serving release, what needs attention. |
| **Logs** | The run log, each deploy's build log, and the app's [events](../../run/events/). |
| **Deployments** | Every deploy, the serving release, rollback. |
| **Usage** | Who opened it and what it consumes. |
| **Automation** | Scheduled tasks and jobs. |
| **Dependencies** | What it installs and what your install's policy says about it. |
| **Integrations** | Where this app may send messages and files. |
| **Reach** | Everything the platform has given this app, in one place. |
| **Access** | Visibility, who it is shared with, and pending requests. |
| **Settings** | Name, source, environment variables, runtime, tags, deletion. |

See [The app page](../../run/the-app-page/) for detail.

## Your own pages

Under **Your account** (in the user menu):

| Tab | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Who you are and your role. |
| **Access** | Everything you can reach on this install. |
| **Storage** | [Storage](../../data/browsing-a-store/) you hold a grant on. |
| **Credentials** | Your [API keys](../../cli/api-keys/). |
| **Security** | Sign-in history, and signing out everywhere. |

## Command palette

Press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux) to search pages, apps and settings.
Word order does not matter: `idle sleep` and `sleep idle` find the same
setting.

## Documentation

**Documentation** links to these guides for your install's version — user,
admin, and CLI & SDK — and to your organization's knowledge base, if your
administrator has chosen one. The knowledge base is also linked in the header.
